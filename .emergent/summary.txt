<product_requirements>
Ara is a gamified video streaming app targeting a broad audience (initially Gen Z). Core features include 5-tab navigation (Home, Live, Explore, Profile, Creator Dashboard), 720p video streaming with watch-time tracking, and HLS/RTMP-based live streaming. A token system (ARACOIN: +1/min watched, +2/Color Pulse choice for viewers; +1/view for creators, 1 ARACOIN = /bin/bash.01) rewards engagement. The Color Pulse feature prompts users to pick colors, tracks choices, and includes a mandatory baseline assessment. Ads are integrated as rotating bars, a scrolling ticker, and dynamic overlays. A Creator Dashboard shows real-time stats and Color Pulse analytics. Recent additions include full-screen video playback, YouTube-style comments for videos, Twitch-style live chat for streams, and Google Drive video support. The UI features a neon gradient theme. A non-intrusive, dismissible waitlist modal and profile picture management for users have also been integrated.
</product_requirements>
